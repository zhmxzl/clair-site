Effective October 12, 2026

In short

Clair is a notes and tasks app for Mac and Android. This policy explains what data Clair processes, where it is stored and how to delete it.

Where data is stored

On your device. Notes, tasks, lists, folders, attachments and assistant chat history are stored locally in the app’s storage. Your AI provider key is kept in the system’s secure storage — the macOS Keychain or Android Keystore.

In your Google Drive (optional). If you sign in with Google in sync settings, Clair keeps a copy of your data in the app’s hidden folder in your Google Drive so it is available on all your devices. The folder belongs to your account; the developer has no access to it.

Google user data

Clair requests a single permission — drive.appdata: access only to its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive. Clair cannot see your other files, email, contacts or any other Google data.

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to store and sync your own Clair data between your devices. It is not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. Clair’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI features

AI features (assistant, text editing, day plan, semantic search) are optional and work only with an AI provider key you enter yourself (for example, OpenRouter or your own server). When you use them, your request and the parts of your notes and tasks needed for the answer are sent directly from your device to the provider you chose — not through the developer. The provider’s own privacy policy governs how it processes this data.

Semantic search is turned on separately, with a warning: when on, the text of each note is sent to the provider to compute search vectors.

Deleting your data

  • Individual notes and tasks — delete them in the app and empty the trash.
  • Data on the device — uninstall the app.
  • The copy in Google Drive — in Google Drive open “Settings → Manage apps”, find Clair and choose “Delete hidden app data”. You can revoke Clair’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Clair is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children’s data.
