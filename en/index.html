Notes and tasks with an AI assistant

Clair is an app for Mac and Android. Free, with no account and no ads. Your data is stored on your device and, if you turn on sync, in your Google Drive.

Mac with Apple Silicon and Android 7+

Notes

An editor with Markdown-style formatting: use the toolbar or type the markup right in the text.

  • Headings, lists, checklists, quotes and images
  • Folders, bookmarks and quick search across everything
  • Links between notes and tasks with “[[”
  • Text is stored as Markdown

Features

Tasks and reminders

Due dates, repeats, steps and color-coded lists. Reminders arrive even when the Clair window is closed.

Calendar and day plan

Calendar and Ahead show what’s coming up. The assistant can help lay out today’s tasks.

Assistant

Answers from your notes and tasks, edits text and proposes changes. Changes are applied only after you confirm.

Sync

Through the app’s hidden folder in your Google Drive — between your Mac and phone.

Appearance

Light, dusk and dark themes, your own accent color, note font and text size.

Works offline

Everything works offline; sync resumes when you’re back online.

Privacy

Clair has no servers or accounts of its own, so the developer never receives your data.

  • Sync uses the app’s hidden folder in your Google Drive; no access to your other files
  • Your AI key is kept in the macOS Keychain and Android Keystore
  • No ads, analytics or trackers

Installing on Mac

Open the .dmg file and drag Clair to Applications. On first launch macOS warns about an unidentified developer — open “System Settings → Privacy & Security” and click “Open Anyway”.

Installing on Android

Open the downloaded .apk file and allow installing from this source when your phone asks. Then it works like any other app.

To check the file wasn’t tampered with, run shasum -a 256 file-name in Terminal and compare with SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page.

Questions

How much does Clair cost?

Nothing. No subscription, ads or paid features, and no sign-up.

Do I have to use AI?

No, notes, tasks, reminders and sync all work without it. The assistant connects with your own OpenRouter key (it has free models too) or your own server, such as Ollama on your computer. You pay the provider directly.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you turn on sync, also in the app’s hidden folder in your Google Drive. Clair sees only that folder, and the developer sees nothing. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why does macOS warn about an unidentified developer?

Avoiding that warning requires a paid Apple signature. Clair is distributed without it, so the first time you need to open it manually. You can verify the file with its checksum.

What about iPhone and Windows?

For now Clair is available for Macs with Apple Silicon and for Android.

How do I update?

When a new version comes out, Clair shows a notice with a Download button. Install it over the old one — your data stays.
