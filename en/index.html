Tasks

A task, in plain words

Clair picks the due date, reminder, repeat, list and tags straight from what you type. No menus to dig through — on your phone too.

  • Steps, files, a note and a colour for every task
  • Repeats like “every Monday” or “3 days after done”

Notes

Notes that connect

A Markdown-style editor: use the toolbar, the “/” menu or type the markup right in the text.

  • Headings, checklists, tables, callouts, code and foldable sections
  • Links between notes and tasks with “[[” — and you see what links back
  • #tags right in the text, nested with “/”
  • Version history — bring back any edit
  • Templates, export to Markdown and PDF

Assistant

AI on your key, with your yes

It answers from your notes and tasks, edits text and breaks tasks into steps. It only suggests changes — nothing is applied until you confirm.

  • An OpenRouter key (free models available) or your own server, like Ollama on your computer
  • Requests go straight to the provider — Clair never sees them
  • Everything else works the same without AI
Privacy

Your data stays yours

Clair has no servers and no accounts, so the developer never gets your data.

  • Sync goes through a hidden app folder in your Google Drive; there’s no access to your other files
  • Your AI key lives in the macOS Keychain and Android Keystore
  • No ads, analytics or trackers

Mac Android

Installing on Mac

Open the .dmg and drag Clair to Applications. On first launch macOS warns about an unidentified developer — open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”.

Installing on Android

Open the downloaded .apk and allow installs from this source when your phone asks. From there it’s a normal app.

To check the file wasn’t tampered with, run shasum -a 256 file-name in Terminal and compare with SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page.

FAQ

The short answers

How much does Clair cost?

Nothing. No subscription, ads or paid features, and no sign-up.

Do I have to connect AI?

No — notes, tasks, reminders and sync all work without it. The assistant uses your own OpenRouter key (it has free models too) or your own server, such as Ollama on your computer. You pay the provider directly.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you turn on sync, also in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. Clair sees only that folder; the developer sees nothing. More in the privacy policy.

Can I bring notes from another app?

Yes: Settings → Sync → Import. Google Keep, Evernote, Todoist and Markdown are supported, including Obsidian, Bear and Notion folders. Importing again doesn’t create duplicates.

Why does macOS warn about an unidentified developer?

Avoiding that warning needs a paid Apple signature. Clair ships without one, so the first time you open it by hand. You can check the file with its checksum.

What about iPhone and Windows?

For now Clair is available for Apple Silicon Macs and Android.

How do I update?

When a new version is out, Clair shows a notice with a Download button. Install it over the old one — your data stays.
